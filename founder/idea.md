# Hess Cafe

- What it is: a morning-only café (6am–12pm) run inside an existing bar, Mamali, at 70 Christopher St, West Village, New York City, with a funky, cute look.
- Who it is for: a mix of West Village locals and commuters on weekdays, and visitors / Instagram-driven customers (tourists) on weekends.
- What it sells, at what price: signature dessert-inspired lattes at $7–8 each — matcha latte, strawberry cheesecake matcha latte, cherry pie matcha latte, halva latte, apple strudel latte — plus a regular coffee menu and a hot chocolate topped with torched (burnt) meringue.
- Where and how: walk-in café in the Mamali bar space, mornings only, before the bar opens. Customers find it by foot traffic and social media (to be confirmed).
- Budget and constraints: start-up budget about $2,000. Cannot change the interior of the bar (any design must be non-permanent / removable). Rent is 25–30% of revenue (a revenue share), with no utilities to pay.
