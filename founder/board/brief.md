# Board brief: Hess Cafe

This brief is everything the board knows. Read it on its own; do not assume anything that is not here. Where you need a fact that is not here, name it as an open question.

## The idea

- Name: Hess Cafe (working name).
- What it is: a morning-only café, open 6am–12pm, operating inside an existing bar called Mamali at 70 Christopher St, West Village, New York City. The bar is the host; the café uses its space in the hours the bar is closed.
- Look: a "funky, cute" design. The founder cannot change the bar's interior, so any design has to be non-permanent and removable (or limited to cups, signage, menu, staff, music, movable pieces).
- Menu:
  - Signature drinks, $7–8 each: matcha latte; strawberry cheesecake matcha latte; cherry pie matcha latte; halva latte; apple strudel latte.
  - Hot chocolate with torched (burnt) meringue on top (price not stated).
  - Regular coffee drinks, espresso-based only, no drip (prices not stated).
  - Pastries (source and prices not stated).
- Customers: a mix — West Village locals and commuters on weekdays; visitors / Instagram-driven customers on weekends.
- Deal with the host: rent is 25–30% of revenue, as a revenue share. No utilities to pay.
- Start-up budget: about $2,000 in total (equipment, décor, first inventory).

## Confirmed after the first board meeting

- Equipment: the bar provides it.
- Signage: the founder may put a frame and posters on the front glass.
- Permit: the founder has the permit.

## Not yet known (open questions)

- Staffing: whether the founder works every shift alone.
- Expected customers per day, cost per drink, competitors nearby.
- Length and terms of the agreement with the bar (term, notice, exclusivity), and who carries liability.
- Where the pastries come from and what they cost.
