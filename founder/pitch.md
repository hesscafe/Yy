Hess Cafe, a new morning café inside Mamali at 70 Christopher St (West Village), at the Christopher St–Stonewall subway exit. A chalkboard on the window says "Open for coffee now" from 6am.
Open 6am to 12pm, six days a week. Takeaway, with a few seats inside.
Signature lattes, $8: strawberry cheesecake matcha, cherry pie matcha, halva latte, apple strudel latte, and hot chocolate with torched meringue. Any signature drink can be made half-sweet. One rotating "drink of the week".
Plain matcha latte $7, with the matcha's grade and farm named on the menu. Free tasting sips of the plain matcha, 6–9am on weekdays.
Regular espresso drinks $6–7.50 (flat white and cortado $6, cappuccino $7.50, latte and iced latte $7.50), chai latte $6, plus a $4 Americano for a quick black coffee. Pastries from Balthazar, $6.
Don't love your drink? We remake it free. Punch card: 10th drink free.
