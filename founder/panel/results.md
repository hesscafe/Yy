## What would flip a no

- Solid reviews saying the plain flat white is excellent and service is fast, or a coworker vouching for it, and I'd try it once on an office day.
- If my usual place closed or had a long line, or a friend told me the flat white there is genuinely better than mine.
- A plain matcha latte at $6 or less, with something saying what grade of matcha they use and where it comes from.
- A friend dragging me there on a weekend and it turning out to have a real place to sit and a drink that's genuinely good, not just pretty.
- A solid Google rating (4.5+ with a decent number of reviews) saying the regular flat white is actually good, not just photogenic.
- Seeing it at 4.5 stars or higher on Google with a decent number of reviews that say the matcha is actually good, plus a clear café sign out front so I know it's open in the morning.
- Telling me the matcha's grade and where it comes from, or a free taste of a plain matcha latte on my way to the train.
- Seeing the strawberry cheesecake or cherry pie matcha on Instagram or TikTok, or a big obvious sign or sandwich board outside saying it's open for coffee right now.

Buyers say they would buy **2.0 times** in the first month on average.
