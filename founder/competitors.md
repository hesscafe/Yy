## Price range

The only matcha latte price found was Matchaful's, at about $12.59 on a delivery app. An unidentified West Village shop lists an [oat matcha at $8.44](https://www.atly.com/united-states/new-york/new-york/west-village/best-chill-latte). Generic guides put NYC specialty espresso drinks at about $5–8. **At $7–8, Hess Cafe's matcha would be cheaper than the nearest matcha shop.** The median and full range can't be computed from what is public.

## What customers complain about

There wasn't enough public review text to rank complaints. The themes below have **fewer than three sources each**:
- **Partners Coffee:** crowded with laptop workers, loud music, can feel unfriendly ([atly](https://www.atly.com/location/PartnersCoffee1)).
- **Aokō:** long waits at peak times, especially weekends ([frankiapp](https://frankiapp.com/business/aoko-matcha-768986)).

## The gap

1. **Matcha before 8:30am.** Matchaful opens at 8:30am and Aokō at 11am. Nobody nearby serves premium matcha to the 6–8:30am commuter.
2. **Dessert-flavoured matcha.** None of the listings found sell cheesecake, cherry pie, apple strudel or halva lattes.
3. **Price.** At $7–8, Hess Cafe would be well below Matchaful's listed matcha latte.

## Threats

- **Mamali itself is the biggest one.** Public listings describe Mamali as a café serving espresso drinks and breakfast, with one listing showing a 7:30am opening. Before you sign, confirm that Mamali will stop serving coffee in the morning and won't compete with you. If the deal ends, Mamali could keep your menu. A [Community Board 2 filing from April 2025](https://cbmanhattan.cityofnewyork.us/cb2/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2025/05/2025-04-17-SLA-Marla-2022-Inc-dba-Mamali-NYC-70-Christopher-St-aka-110-7th-Avenue-South-1.pdf) recommended denying Mamali's wine licence. Ask the owners about the space's licensing status.
- **Matchaful** could copy a flavoured latte or open earlier quickly, since it is a block away.
- **Foot traffic:** Christopher St–Stonewall station had about [3.36 million riders in 2015](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Christopher_Street%E2%80%93Stonewall_station) (about 9,200 a day). That figure is old, and no current one was found. Mamali sits on the station's corner.
