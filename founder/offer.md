## The stack

**Name: "The 6am Matcha on Christopher St."** Dessert-flavoured matcha you can try for free, at the subway exit, two hours before anyone else nearby opens for matcha.

- **The core:** a signature dessert latte or torched-meringue hot chocolate, $7.50, made to look good and taste good. Half-sweet on request.
- **Bonus 1, the free taste:** sips of the plain matcha latte, 6–9am weekdays. The menu names the grade and farm. This answers "is the matcha good?" before anyone pays.
- **Bonus 2, the commuter lane:** a $4 Americano and regular espresso drinks served first during the rush.
- **Bonus 3, the punch card:** 10th drink free, with double stamps for the first 2 weeks.
- **Guarantee:** "Don't love your drink? We remake it free."
- **Real urgency:** the drink of the week changes every week. Opening double stamps end after 2 weeks.

## What it costs

- **Remake guarantee:** a remake costs only ingredients (about $1–2; rent is 0 on a free drink). If 3% of 50 drinks a day are remade, that's 1.5 remakes, about **$2–3 a day**.
- **Free tasting sips:** if 40 sips a day are 1 oz each, that's about 3–4 drinks' worth of matcha and milk, roughly **$3–4 a day** on weekdays. Stop after the first 2–4 weeks, or once reviews are in.
- **Punch card:** about $1 per 10 drinks, so **about $0.10 per drink**.
- **$7.50 instead of $8:** about **$0.33 less per signature drink**.
- **Americano at $4:** leaves about $1.81 (beans $0.55, cup $0.24, rent $1.20, fee $0.20).

All of these use the founder's cost sheet. The signature drinks' extra cost is still an estimate ($0.50–1.00).

## Re-test result

Same 20 simulated buyers (seed 320801), shown the new pitch (`pitch.md`, old one in `pitch-v1.md`). Results in `panel-v2/results.md`.

| | Before (v1) | After (v2) |
|---|---|---|
| Buy | 12 of 20 (60%) | **13 of 20 (65%)** |
| Weekday commuters who buy | 2 of 7 | **3 of 7** |
| Total purchases in the first month | 24 | **31 (+29%)** |
| Median "getting expensive" | $7.25 | $7.50 |
| Signature price vs. acceptable range | $8 = top edge | $7.50, inside ($4.02–$7.99) |

**Who switched to yes:** P002 (commuter, took the free sip), P017 (Matchaful regular, wants to compare the named matcha), P019 (weekend visitor).
**Who switched to no:** P008 (price-conscious weekend visitor: the tasting is weekdays only, and every special drink is still $7+), P020 (commuter, loyal to their usual café).

**What worked:** naming the matcha and the free tasting moved matcha fans and commuters. Buyers now come more often (about 1.5 → 2+ times a month).
**What did not move:** "reads every review" buyers still wait for a Google rating, people who want to sit down still pass, and some still doubt that half-sweet fixes the sweetness. These are about the market and the space, not the offer. Reviews only come with time.

**Next tweaks to test:** a weekend tasting sip too (P008), photos of the drinks on Instagram before opening (P011, P015), and asking early customers for Google reviews.
