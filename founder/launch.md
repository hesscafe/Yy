# Launch plan: Hess Cafe

**Proposed dates** (no target date was given, so this is the earliest realistic one; change it if you need to):
- **Soft open (friends and family): Saturday, November 28, 2026**
- **Public opening (L): Monday, November 30, 2026**
- The holiday season brings more weekend visitors to the West Village.

## 1. The test: the first 2 weeks *are* the test

Rent is a share of sales and the equipment is Mamali's, so opening costs very little. Instead of a separate pop-up, run the **first 2 weeks (Nov 30 – Dec 12) as the test**, at real prices, with the cheapest possible décor (posters and a chalkboard only). Spend the rest of the décor budget only if it passes.

**Success line (written before opening):**

| Measure | Pass | Rethink |
|---|---|---|
| Average drinks per day, over 12 days | **40 or more** (≈ $3,200/month left before tax, at 26 days and ~$3.07 a drink) | Under 25 |
| Weekday average | 30+ | Under 20 |
| Saturday average | 60+ | Under 35 |
| Customers coming back (punch cards with 2+ stamps by day 12) | 1 in 4 | Under 1 in 10 |
| Signature drinks as a share of all drinks | Just record it | |

**Compare with the panel:** 13 of 20 simulated buyers said they'd buy, about 2.4 times in the first month. Simulated buyers are optimistic. **If the real numbers fall well short, trust the real numbers** and go back to `/founder-offer` or `/founder-pricing` before spending more.

Also test the signature price: **$7.50 in week 1, $8 in week 2**, and compare signature drinks per day (`pricing.md`).

**If something breaks** (from the risk register in `ops.md`):
- **Machine down:** serve matcha and pastries only, sign "espresso back tomorrow", post it.

## 4. The first 30 days

**Track daily:** drinks by type, pastries sold and wasted, weekday vs. Saturday, new vs. punch-card customers, Google reviews.

| Review | Date | Questions to answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 7** | Sun Dec 6 | Average drinks/day so far vs. the 40 line? Is the rush under 2 minutes? What do people order most? Which hook brought people in? Pastry waste? |
| **Day 14 (test result)** | Sun Dec 13 | **Pass or rethink** (table above)? $7.50 vs. $8: which sold more signature drinks per day, and which left more money? Reviews: 10+? Decide: buy the rest of the décor, change the menu, or rethink. |
| **Day 30** | Tue Dec 29 | Real monthly numbers → `/founder-cfo` with real volume. Keep, cut or add signature drinks. Stop the free tastings? Is it worth the hours for two people? |

**Trigger levels:** weekdays under 20 drinks by day 14, or under 10 Google reviews by day 14, means change something (the window, the commuter hooks, ask for reviews every day). See `marketing.md`.
