Every number below comes from the input file. Nothing is looked up or guessed.

## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $4,140 a month (Rent: flat $4,000/month to Mamali $4,000, General liability insurance (ESTIMATE, get a quote) $80, Cleaning supplies, napkins, butane, punch cards (ESTIMATE) $60, Wages (founder + mom, unpaid) $0, Utilities (included by Mamali) $0).

- **Break-even: 30 drinks a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (60 a day): **41%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 150 a day.
