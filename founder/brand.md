# Brand: Hess Cafe

Built from the positioning in `marketing.md` (morning matcha bar at the Christopher St subway exit, dessert-flavoured matcha from 6am), what buyers said in `panel/results.md`, and competitors to stay clear of in `competitors.md`.

## The name

You already chose **Hess Cafe**. Ten options are listed for comparison:

| Name | Style | What it says | Out loud / on a sign | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hess Cafe** | Founder's name | Personal, short | One syllable, easy, fits a cup | "Hess" is a well-known gas-station brand (Hess Corporation). Different business, but check trademarks. Says nothing about matcha. |
| Hess Morning | Founder + promise | Mornings only | Clear | Long for a 32px icon |
| Six to Noon | Descriptive | Hours as the idea | Memorable, explains the pop-up | Hours may change |
| Pink & Green | Evocative | Strawberry + matcha | Fun, photogenic | Generic, hard to own |
| Cherry Whisk | Evocative | Dessert + matcha | Cute, sounds like a café | May be taken |
| Sweet Exit | Place + treat | At the subway exit | Playful | Could read oddly |
| Christopher Matcha | Place | Where + what | Clear | Close to "Matchaful" on the same street |
| Matchamorning | Descriptive | What + when | Easy | Generic; matcha names are crowded |
| Halva & Whisk | Evocative | Signature drink | Distinctive | Hard to spell |
| Little Torch | Evocative | The torched meringue | Memorable, visual | Doesn't say coffee |

**Shortlist:** Hess Cafe (keep), Six to Noon, Cherry Whisk.

**Recommendation:** keep **Hess Cafe** as the name and use a sub-line on signs, like **"Hess Cafe · matcha from 6am"**. That fixes "says nothing about matcha" without a rename.

### Checks for you to finish

- **Confusion:** a web search found no "Hess Cafe" in New York. The closest was [Cafe HESED](https://www.atly.com/location/CafeHESED). Avoid anything close to "Matchaful".
- **Trademark:** search [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for "HESS", "HESS CAFE" and each shortlisted name in **Class 43** (café and restaurant services), **Class 30** (coffee, tea, matcha drinks) and **Class 32** (non-alcoholic drinks). "HESS" is likely registered for fuel and gas stations. A different class usually means less risk, but a well-known mark can still object, so ask a trademark lawyer before printing much.
- **Domain:** check whether hesscafe.com / hesscafe.nyc are free at any registrar (not checked here). Alternatives: hesscafenyc.com, hessmorning.com.
- **Handles:** check @hesscafe and @hesscafenyc on Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube, and grab the same handle everywhere.

I can only run public searches. A trademark lawyer confirms it, and nothing is registered until you register it.

## Promise, tagline and voice

**The promise:** *A good matcha at 6am, made sweet the way you like it, or we make it again.*

**Tagline options**
1. **"Matcha from 6am."** *(recommended: clear, answers "is it open?")*
2. "Dessert you can drink, before work."
3. "Six to noon, pink and green."

**Voice**

| Adjective | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Warm | "Morning! Want it half-sweet?" | "Please place your order." |
| Playful | "Cherry pie, but make it matcha." | "OMG the CUTEST drinks EVER!!!" |
| Honest | "Ceremonial matcha from [farm]. Taste it first." | "The best matcha in New York." (can't prove it) |

## The look (brief)

**Colours** (contrast checked against WCAG AA, which needs 4.5:1 for text)

| Colour | Hex | Job | Text contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matcha green | `#2F5D3A` | Logo, headings, the "open" sign | Green on cream **7.0:1** ✅ |
| Cream | `#FFF4E6` | Background, cups, menu | Dark ink `#1E1E1E` on cream **15.4:1** ✅ |
| Strawberry pink | `#F4A7B9` | The "funky, cute" accent: stickers, posters, cup band | Dark ink on pink **8.8:1** ✅. Don't put cream or white text on it. |
| Cherry (accent) | `#B3264A` | Prices, "drink of the week" | Cherry on cream **5.9:1** ✅ |

**Type** (free, open licence, from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com))
- Display: **Fraunces**, a soft, slightly playful serif for the logo and drink names.
- Text: **DM Sans**, clean and readable for the menu and prices.

**Logo brief**
- Must say: Hess Cafe, plus the morning-matcha feel. Friendly, a little funky.
- Must work: on the window poster, a cup sticker (you can't print cups yet), a chalkboard drawn by hand, and a 32px Instagram avatar.
- Idea to explore: a small whisk or a matcha cup with a little torch flame or sun at 6am. Green on cream, with a pink sticker version.
- Avoid: anything looking like Matchaful's style, a gas-station "H", or too much detail to read small.
- No image tool is attached to this session. If you make concepts elsewhere, treat them as concepts, not a finished logo.

**Inside a bar you can't change:** everything must be removable. Use pink and green window posters, a cream table runner on the bar top, a small pink-and-green pennant line, stickers on plain cups, a chalkboard, and morning music. It all goes in a box at noon.

## The first five touchpoints

1. **The window at 6am:** a chalkboard with "Open for coffee now · Matcha from 6am", the drink of the week, and posters showing the 5 drinks with prices.
2. **The cup:** a plain white cup with a pink Hess Cafe sticker and a hand-written "½" when it's half-sweet.
3. **The punch card:** cream card, green stamp, "10th drink's on us", plus the Instagram handle.
4. **The first post:** the torch hitting the meringue, captioned "Something pink and green is coming to 70 Christopher St. 6am."
5. **The reply to the first complaint:** "Sorry it wasn't right. Come by and we'll remake it, any drink, on us. — [your name]". Reply within a day, publicly, and keep it short.
