Hess Cafe, a new morning café inside Mamali at 70 Christopher St (West Village), at the Christopher St–Stonewall subway exit.
Open 6am to 12pm, six days a week. Takeaway, with a few seats inside the bar space.
Signature lattes, $8: strawberry cheesecake matcha, cherry pie matcha, halva latte, apple strudel latte, and hot chocolate with torched meringue.
Regular espresso drinks $5–7 (flat white, cortado, cappuccino, latte, iced latte), matcha latte $7, chai latte $6. No drip coffee.
Pastries from Balthazar, $5–6.
