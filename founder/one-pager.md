# Hess Cafe: one page

**Verdict: Profitable, on a guessed 60 drinks a day** (to be confirmed by the 2-week test). 12 of 20 simulated buyers buy (60%).

**The problem.** In the West Village, nobody near the Christopher St subway exit sells premium or dessert-flavoured matcha before 8:30am. Matchaful, a block away, opens at 8:30am, and its matcha latte is listed at about $12.59.

**The customer.** West Village locals and weekend visitors who want a matcha worth the walk and the photo. Second audience: 1-train commuters who want a quick coffee.

**The offer.** "The 6am Matcha on Christopher St." Dessert-flavoured matcha (cheesecake, cherry pie, halva, apple strudel) and torched-meringue hot chocolate at $8, half-sweet on request. Plain matcha $7, with the grade and farm named and free tasting sips 6–9am on weekdays. Espresso drinks $6–7.50, chai $6, a $4 Americano, Balthazar pastries $6. A free remake if you don't love it, a punch card (10th drink free) and a drink of the week.

**The evidence.**
- Buyer panel (simulated): 12 of 20 buy. 13 of 20 with the improved offer, and first-month purchases rose from 24 to 31. Residents buy 5 of 6, commuters 3 of 7.
- Competitor gap: no early-morning dessert matcha nearby, and a lower price than Matchaful.
- Board: 3 fund if, 0 pass (average 5.7 / 10), on conditions: a signed deal with Mamali, a real named matcha, and a short launch menu.
- Real test: **not yet**. The first 2 weeks after opening (Nov 30 – Dec 12, 2026), with a pass line of 40+ drinks a day.

**What the money is for.** The ~$2,000 covers first stock (matcha, beans, milk, cups, syrups), a torch, a Square reader, about $300 of marketing (posters, chalkboard, punch cards, free drinks), and insurance and registration fees. No big spend until the test passes.

**The ask.** No outside money needed. The founder needs from Mamali a signed agreement: a term and notice period, Mamali not selling coffee in the morning, the equipment and repairs, and the recipes and brand staying the founder's.
